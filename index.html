Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MyRingReply.

Can I keep my existing business number? +
Yes! We can forward your existing number or provide you with a new one. You choose what works best for your business. We handle all the technical setup for you.
What if I'm already on a call when another customer calls? +
That's exactly when MyRingReply shines! We instantly capture that missed call, send an SMS to the caller, and alert you — so no lead slips away while you're helping another customer.
Can I customize the SMS template? +
Absolutely! You can personalize the message, add your branding, or adjust the tone to match your business voice. We'll help you craft the perfect message during onboarding.
How fast is setup? +
Most clients are up and running within 24 hours. We handle all the technical setup for you — just provide your business details and we'll take care of the rest.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes! No long-term contracts. Cancel anytime with 30 days notice. We'll even help you export your data so you keep everything.
Is this TCPA compliant? +
Yes! We follow all FCC regulations. Every message includes an opt-out option, and we only send messages to people who called you. Your customers can reply "STOP" at any time to opt out.
What happens if the customer replies to the text? +
You get a notification instantly! We capture all replies and send them to you in real-time. You can respond directly from your phone or the dashboard.
Do you offer a free trial? +
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all plans. Try us risk-free — if you're not satisfied, get a full refund. No questions asked.
