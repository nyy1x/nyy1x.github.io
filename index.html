Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How fast does the SMS response go out?
Our system sends the SMS within 2-3 seconds of the missed call. This instant response makes your business look professional and responsive.
What if I don't have a phone number for the caller?
We capture the caller's phone number automatically from the missed call. If it's a blocked number, we'll still notify you about the missed call.
Can I customize the SMS templates?
Yes! Every client gets custom SMS templates tailored to their business. You can edit them anytime through your dashboard.
Is MyRingReply compliant with TCPA regulations?
Yes! We follow all TCPA guidelines. Every SMS includes an opt-out option, and we ensure all messages comply with current regulations.
How long does setup take?
Most clients are up and running within 10-15 minutes. We handle all the technical setup for you — just provide your business info and we'll do the rest.
What happens if I cancel?
You can cancel anytime. We'll export all your lead data and help you transition smoothly. No lock-in contracts.
